First serve
{{/* One rally decides it at the table; the winner's side presses, and that press scores nothing (ADR-0005). */}}
  • Left / Right: that player serves first.
  • Play for it: one rally decides; the winner presses their button and serves first. That press scores no point.
Before the first point
{{/* Both sides press once when they are ready. */}}
  • Warm-up: knock up first; nothing counts until both players have pressed their button once.
  • Straight in: the first press is already a point.
Ends
{{/* After every set, and at five in the deciding set; play waits until each player has pressed at the new half. */}}
  • Stay: each player keeps their side of the table for the whole match.
  • Change: players swap sides after every set, and at 5 points in the deciding set, as the rules say. Play pauses until both have pressed the button on their new side.
